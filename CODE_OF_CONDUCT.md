# Code of Conduct

Claude Switch is a friendly project. Everyone who takes part (issues, pull requests, discussions)
is expected to:

- be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or identity;
- give and take feedback on the work, not the person;
- assume good intent, and ask before assuming;
- keep discussions on topic and free of harassment, insults and spam.

Unacceptable behaviour can lead to comments being removed and people being blocked from the project.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Ngoni-Sama). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
